## Design Context

### Users

Quackback serves product and support teams collecting, triaging, prioritizing, and closing the loop on customer feedback. The admin interface primarily supports daily power users moving frequently between feedback, support, publishing, analytics, automation, user, and configuration surfaces.

### Brand Personality

Efficient, calm, and capable. The product should feel focused and approachable without sacrificing the density expected from an operational tool.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use a refined utilitarian product aesthetic in the quackback.io visual language, in light and dark themes: ink on warm beige, or warm white on ink. Favor compact navigation, clear state changes, restrained color, and familiar controls over decorative UI.

The admin palette, type and focus ring live in `apps/web/src/styles/admin-theme.css`, scoped to the admin shell. Customer surfaces (the portal, the widget, portal sign-in) keep the workspace's own branding and never take admin values.

- Ink is the primary colour. Primary buttons are ink with canvas text; duck yellow (`#f7cc29`) is an accent only: count badges, the focus halo, the active rail icon in dark mode. Never a button fill or a text colour on beige.
- Raised objects (cards, popovers, fields, menus, dialogs) sit on `--card`, the warm-white surface over the beige page. Separate surfaces with hairlines, not shadows.
- No blue, purple, amber or emerald UI colours. Status reads as an icon plus a label; danger and warning fills are tints with ink text, and the only solid danger fill is the destructive button. A workspace's own colour (a status, a tag) shows only as a dot.
- One data series is ink; two to four use `--chart-1` to `--chart-4`, always with labels.
- Headings for pages, panels and dialogs use Bricolage Grotesque; everything else uses Figtree.
- Copy: sentence case, no exclamation marks, "…" not "...", "Clear filters".

### Design Principles

- Optimize admin workflows for repeated daily use and fast navigation.
- Keep related controls compact while preserving clear focus, hover, and active states.
- Use the existing component primitives, typography, color tokens, and spacing scale.
- Preserve comfortable touch targets on mobile; apply higher density where pointer input is expected.
- Prefer quiet hierarchy and purposeful spacing over decorative containers or effects.
